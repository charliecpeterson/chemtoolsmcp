# Exercises - Relativistic Quantum Chemistry (TCCM 2021)

These exercises are based on the course given by Trond Saue for The
European Master in Theoretical Chemistry and Computational Modelling
(TCCM).

This electronic web material can be part of any workshops on
relativistic quantum chemistry, where theory lessons are coupled with
DIRAC computer exercises.

Before embarking on these exercises, it is strongly recommended to
review the lectures notes ! Further theoretical background, in
particular about Hamiltonians, is found in Trond Saue, ChemPhysChem
**12** (2011) 3077
([pdf](https://hal.archives-ouvertes.fr/hal-00662643v1)).
