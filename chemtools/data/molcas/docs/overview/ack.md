# 1.1.5. Acknowledgment¶

The Molcas project is carried out by the Lund University quantum chemistry group supported by the Swedish Science Research Council (VR).

The MOLCAS group is a member of the Linnaeus project _Organising Molecular Matter, OMM_.

The MOLCAS group acknowledges the contributions from the scientists in the MOLCAS Network, who are members of the MOLCAS developers team.

The Lund University Center for Scientific and Technical Computing (LUNARC) has provided computer resources for the project.
